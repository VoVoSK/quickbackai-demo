Closing the feedback loop in recruitment with AI

When a hiring process closes, the hiring manager automatically gets a one-minute card in Teams. AI turns the answers into concrete actions for HR.

TodayFeedback happens only in conversations, e-mails and ad hoc chats. It is informal, gets lost or isn’t complete, so the same problems repeat.
With QuickbackAIA trigger event starts everything, managers answer in about a minute, and AI suggests what to change before the next search.

Step 4 · Actionable insights

AI analysis

Copilot removes names, tags the topics and their tone, and gives each answer a one-sentence summary. Nobody reads and sorts comments by hand.

  • Names are removed before anything is stored
  • Fixed topic list, so results can be counted
  • One-sentence summary per answer

QuickbackAI in one picture
An automatic trigger, a one-minute Teams card, AI analysis, a dashboard and concrete actions, all in Microsoft 365.

    
  

  

    
Automation
A trigger event starts everything. Recruiters do nothing extra.
    
      
  • Closing a position starts the flow
  • Positions that never close: asked after each hire
  • Closest open window in Outlook
  • No window within 3 days: sent anyway

    
Good experience
About one minute, inside Teams, with questions that fit the outcome.
    
      
  • 3 ratings and 1 tailored question
  • Rating scale can be tweaked
  • Facts pre-filled from the tracker
  • At most one smart follow-up

    
Actionable insights
AI finds patterns and suggests what to change before the next search.
    
      
  • Names removed, topics and tone tagged
  • AI summary of every answer for the recruiter
  • Dashboard and monthly Copilot summary
  • Lessons for the next search
